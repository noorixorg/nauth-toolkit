# License

**nauth-toolkit is free and open source software, released under the [MIT License](https://github.com/noorixorg/nauth-toolkit/blob/main/LICENSE).**

Every package in the toolkit — the core engine, the framework adapters, the client SDKs, and all optional providers — is published under the same MIT terms. There is no separate "pro" tier, no early-access gate, no per-user pricing, and no commercial license to purchase.

## What you can do

- **Use it for anything** — commercial products, internal tools, side projects, anything in between.
- **Modify and fork it** — read every line, change what you need, ship your fork.
- **Redistribute it** — bundle it, sublicense it, or sell software built on it.
- **Self-host everything** — auth runs in your process, against your database, with no external calls.

The only requirement is that the copyright notice and permission notice are included in copies or substantial portions of the software.
